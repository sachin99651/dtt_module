Process

How it works

01

Browser Camera

Your device camera is accessed via the browser — works on any phone, tablet or desktop.

02

Frame Upload

Frames are captured on-device and sent to the server 10× per second as compressed JPEG.

03

Hand Landmark Detection

Google MediaPipe extracts 21 3D hand landmarks per frame — fingertips, knuckles, and wrist positions.

04

CNN Inference

75 consecutive frames are stacked and classified by the trained Conv1D model with a confidence score.

05

Result Display

High-confidence predictions are shown on-screen in real time and cleared after 3 seconds.
